Frequently Asked Questions

Q

Can we contract via 나라장터 / 학교장터?

A

Yes, contracts can be made through 나라장터 and 학교장터 (Korean public procurement platforms).

Q

Can we pay after the class ends?

A

Yes, post-class payment is supported.

Q

Recommend fun programs that don't require a PC.

A

3D Pen, Drones, Autonomous Driving, YouTube Creator, Renewable Energy, LED Mood Lamp, and more.

Q

We want classes where students build and take home a project.

A

We recommend Singing LED Mood Lamp with ChatGPT, Smart Home Expert, IR-Sensor Dog Car, Bluetooth Speaker, Ultrasonic Sensor Robot Trash Can, and Night View Designer.

  • A beam projector and Wi-Fi must be available at the venue.
  • Teaching materials must be returned after class; damages are billable.
  • Curriculum may be adjusted upon request by the school or institution.
