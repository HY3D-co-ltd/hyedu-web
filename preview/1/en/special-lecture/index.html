About the Lecture

The 4th Industrial Revolution Lecture

A lecture for developing convergent, creative talent to lead the 4th Industrial Revolution era.
Through a specialist instructor, students understand the 4IR and explore its flagship technologies.
It also helps broaden students\u2019 career-planning horizons.

Program Overview

Target
  • K-12 students aspiring to be startup-makers for the 4th Industrial Revolution
  • Schools needing integrated programs for students, parents, and teachers
  • Schools planning phased programs on career, learning, and character
  • Schools introducing diverse linked programs — lectures, workshops, training, mentoring
  • Local governments or education offices supporting region-specific integrated programs for local schools
CapacityMinimum 20 per session (fewer than 20 → per-person fee increases)
FeeKRW 20,000 / person (VAT excl.)
Duration90 min / 120 min
VenueSchools, youth centers, culture centers, training centers, universities, etc.
ContactHanyang Future Lab / +82-70-8064-0829

Program Flow

Intro

What is the 4th Industrial Revolution?

  • The emergence of the 4IR
  • From the 1st to the 4th Industrial Revolution
  • Why we need to prepare for the 4IR
Develop

4IR Technologies

  • Explore core 4IR technologies: 3D printing, AI, VR/AR
  • Predict the future of the 4IR era
Wrap-up

Preparing Ourselves for the 4IR

  • Compare how individuals and companies prepare for the 4IR
  • Grow entrepreneurial spirit to thrive in the 4IR

Regional Project Activation
