About the Camp

Hanyang Convergence Engineering Camp

An exploration- and experience-driven camp that develops convergent, creative talent for the 4th Industrial Revolution.
Grounded in an entrepreneurial mindset, students understand and experience the principles of representative future technologies and build their inquiry skills.
Along with entrepreneurship education, students explore future careers enabled by 4th Industrial Revolution technologies, broadening their career perspectives.

Program Guide

Core Programs (base)

4th Industrial Revolution & Youth Entrepreneurship

Understand entrepreneurship concepts, learn entrepreneurial mindset, and discuss the future of industry through Q&A.

Relationship Training

Individual personality assessment followed by various team-building activities that foster consideration, coexistence, and relationship-building.

PBL Class for Convergent Creative Talent

A Project Based Learning class where students find their own solutions to real-life problems.

Hanyang University Campus Tour

Campus tour at ERICA Campus guided by Hanyang University student ambassadors "Saranghandae".

Maker Activities (choose 2)

3D Creations Through Design Thinking

Generate ideas with creative thinking techniques and produce individual works using real 3D printers.

My Life Motto Designed with a 3D Pen

Learn how 3D pens work, then design and build an individual piece expressing your life motto.

Your Own Profile Video Using VR

Shoot a personal profile video with a 360° camera, then build a VR cardboard viewer to watch it.

Interactive Video Content with AR

Produce interactive videos that respond to the viewer and experience the content through an AR hologram viewer.

STEAM Education with Drones

Experience basic hovering and compare drones with and without barometers, then complete missions in teams.

Robot Coding to Build Convergent Thinking

Understand software concepts through mind mapping and control a coding robot via smartphone, app, and command cards.

Hands-on AI Autonomous Driving Engineering

Learn autonomous-driving algorithms through coding and directly control the car via communications to strengthen problem-solving skills.

FAQ

QHow is camp safety managed?▼
A

We are youth camp specialists with extensive safety-management know-how. Our operations team monitors every aspect of the students' camp life. 1) Dormitory: Male and female students are separated and teachers stay on the same floor to supervise. 2) Meals: Served at Hanyang University's dining hall. 3) Health: Mentors check students' health at morning and evening roll call, provide over-the-counter medicine or arrange hospital visits if needed. Every student is covered by youth-activity liability insurance and we maintain a direct line to the nearest emergency room. 4) Daily care: Cleaning, showering, and other routines are checked daily at roll call. 5) Peer relations: Preventive education to avoid bullying and conflicts is provided.

QWhat is the camp accommodation like?▼
A

Students stay in the dormitories of Hanyang University ERICA Campus. Each room has a bathroom, shower, wardrobe, shoe cabinet, desk, and bed. Bedding and HVAC are provided, and mentors check hygiene and other conditions at morning and evening roll call, so students can stay comfortably and safely.

QWho are the mentors running the camp and teaching the classes?▼
A

All instructors hold teaching certifications and have completed 200–300+ hours of training. Life mentors are current students or alumni of Hanyang University ERICA selected from diverse engineering and non-engineering backgrounds. Selected mentors complete a separate mentoring program before joining the camp.

QWhat personal items should I bring?▼
A

Please bring the following. 1) Personal USB drive (8 GB or larger) 2) Writing tools 3) Extra clothes, underwear, and socks 4) Toiletries (towel, toothbrush, toothpaste, soap) 5) Folding umbrella (in case of rain) 6) Phone charger 7) A small bag to carry writing tools and textbooks during the camp.
