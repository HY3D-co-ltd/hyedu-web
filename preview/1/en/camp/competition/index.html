About the Camp

Premium Competition Prep Camp

A premium track combining Hanyang Future Lab's years of curriculum know-how with award-winning instructors experienced in competing and coaching.

A hands-on curriculum and personalized feedback help students prepare effectively for competitions.
Each student can build a unique portfolio item and accumulate experience tailored to their needs — growing into competitive talent suited for the AI era.

Program Overview

TargetOpen to anyone with basic coding experience, from 3rd-grade elementary students through adults.
CapacityMinimum 12 participants (a class may be canceled if fewer than 12 register).
ScheduleBiweekly Saturdays 11:00 a.m. – 5:00 p.m., 4 sessions (6 hours/day, 24 hours total)
LocationHanyang University ERICA Campus (55 Hanyangdaehak-ro, Sangnok-gu, Ansan, Gyeonggi-do)

Why our Competition Prep Camp?

- Proven Instructors

Premium instructors with domestic or international competition wins (or coaching experience) personally guide students through strategies and know-how needed in real competitions.

- Hands-on Curriculum

Beyond theory: robot building, autonomous-driving experiments, smart-city simulations, app and game development — real coding and making that grow self-directed problem-solving and a sense of achievement.

- Personalized Growth Support

Project designs and feedback tailored to each student’s interests and level — beyond mere completion, an opportunity to discover “your own portfolio item” and grow.

- Direct Competition Prep

Building on coding fundamentals, students train with algorithm optimization, presentation prep, and mock competitions — building the capacity to perform confidently in real events.

- Weekend & Vacation Intensives

For busy students juggling school and academies — a 4-session program over weekends or vacation breaks. Short on time, big on results.
