About the Camp

Hanyang Future Saturday Youth Camp

Hanyang Future Lab's long-running B2B program, [Hanyang Youth Camp], has received many inquiries from individual participants.
To provide individuals the same opportunity, we have relaunched it as a “One-day Convergence Tech Intensive Program”.

The program aims to develop convergent, creative talent, letting students dive into AI, maker technology, VR, and coding in a single intensive day.

Why the Hanyang Future Saturday Youth Camp?

- Big results in a short time

A single Saturday of intensive, hands-on learning broadens multi-field understanding and delivers deep convergence knowledge quickly — and in a fun way.

- Theory combined with hands-on practice

Not lectures alone: PBL-based, problem-solving projects combine theory, practice, and making — building real experience, applied skills, and a sense of achievement.

- Leave with a great portfolio addition

After the camp you receive online/offline deliverables and a photo-inclusive certificate that you can use in personal statements and career applications.

- Explore future careers

If you want to explore your interests but aren't ready for a month-long academy, or don't yet know which 4th-IR field fits you — use this camp to set a direction for your career path.
